# How to Get Gap Sense on Your Phone

A short guide for people taking part in the study.

Short sentences. Simple words. Written to be read with a screen reader, or read
aloud to you. Installing takes about two minutes.

---

## If you have an iPhone

The app is shared through **TestFlight**. TestFlight is Apple's own app for
trying apps before they are in the App Store. It is free and safe.

### Step 1. Get TestFlight

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **TestFlight**.
3. Press **Get**. It is free.

If you already have TestFlight, skip this step.

### Step 2. Open the Gap Sense link

Your instructor will send you a link. It begins with
`https://testflight.apple.com/join/`.

1. Open the link on your iPhone. You can tap it in a message or an email.
2. TestFlight opens.
3. Press **Accept**.
4. Press **Install**.

Gap Sense now appears on your home screen. Open it like any other app.

### Step 3. Turn on vibration

1. Open **Gap Sense**.
2. Find the **person** button at the top of the screen and open **Accessibility**.
3. Turn on **Vibrate on cue**.
4. Press **Test haptic pulse**. You feel one knock, then three knocks, then one
   long buzz. Those are the three signals the app uses.

### Good to know

- **The silent switch.** The app plays its sounds even when the side switch is
  on silent. If you hear nothing, check the volume buttons.
- **The app expires after 90 days.** This is a TestFlight rule, not a fault.
  If it stops opening, tap the link again and press **Install** again.
- **Updates.** When a new version is ready, TestFlight tells you. Open TestFlight
  and press **Update**.
- **TestFlight may ask for feedback.** You can ignore that. Your instructor will
  ask you in person.

## If you have an Android phone

The app comes as a file called **GapSense-android-debug.apk**. Your instructor
will send you a link to it.

1. Open the link on your phone. The file downloads.
2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone may say
   **"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps"**.
3. Press **Settings**, then turn on **Allow from this source**.
4. Go back and press **Install**.
5. Press **Open**.

Then turn on vibration the same way as on iPhone: open **Accessibility** from
the person button and turn on **Vibrate on cue**.

Android phones vary. If you get stuck, ask your instructor. It is a one-minute
fix in person.

---

## If you have no smartphone, or you cannot install

You can still try the app in a web browser on any phone or computer. Your
instructor has the address. Everything works except vibration.

---

## Your privacy

The app has no account and no sign-in. It sends nothing anywhere. Your practice
history stays on your own phone. You can delete it at any time from
**Progress**, then **Delete all my practice history**.

The full privacy policy is here:
<https://github.com/meazizul/Gap-Sense-Crossing-Practice/blob/main/PRIVACY.md>
