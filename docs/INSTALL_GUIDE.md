# How to Get CrossingTime on Your Phone

A short guide for people taking part in the study. The shortest version is one
page with a button for each phone: <https://azizulhaque.me/crossingtime/>.

Short sentences. Simple words. Written to be read with a screen reader, or read
aloud to you. Installing takes about two minutes.

---

## If you have an iPhone

The app is shared through **TestFlight**. TestFlight is Apple's own app for
trying apps before they are in the App Store. It is free and safe.

### Step 1. Get TestFlight

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **TestFlight**.
3. Press **Get**. It is free.

If you already have TestFlight, skip this step.

### Step 2. Open the CrossingTime link

The link is:

**<https://testflight.apple.com/join/wYreYpF6>**

1. Open the link on your iPhone. You can tap it in a message or an email, or
   type it into Safari.
2. TestFlight opens.
3. Press **Accept**.
4. Press **Install**.

CrossingTime now appears on your home screen. Open it like any other app.

### Step 3. Check vibration

1. Open **CrossingTime**. The first time, it tells you the app is for use with
   your O&M Specialist. Press **I understand**.
2. Find the **person** button at the top of the screen. It opens
   **Accessibility**.
3. **Vibrate on cue** is already on.
4. Press **Test haptic pulse**. You feel one knock, then three knocks, then one
   long buzz. Those are the three signals the app uses.

### Good to know

- **The silent switch.** The app plays its sounds even when the side switch is
  on silent. If you hear nothing, check the volume buttons.
- **The app expires after 90 days.** This is a TestFlight rule, not a fault.
  If it stops opening, tap the link again and press **Install** again.
- **Updates.** When a new version is ready, TestFlight tells you. Open TestFlight
  and press **Update**. To check your version, open **Help** in the app and
  read **About**.
- **A link from your instructor.** If your instructor sends you a setup link,
  tap it. If it opens in Safari, choose **Open in the CrossingTime app**.
- **TestFlight may ask for feedback.** You can ignore that. Your instructor will
  ask you in person.

## If you have an Android phone

The app is on **Google Play**, for invited testers. Give the email of your
Google account to your instructor or to Azizul Haque; it is added to the list
within a day. Then, on your phone, signed in to that Google account:

**<https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.gapsense.crossingpractice>**

1. Open the link. A Google Play page opens.
2. Press **Become a tester**. You do this once.
3. Press **Download it on Google Play**, then **Install**.
4. Press **Open**. Updates come through Google Play like any other app.

If Google Play says you are not a tester yet, your email is not on the list
yet. Ask your instructor.

**Without Google Play.** The app also comes as a file,
<https://github.com/meazizul/crossingtime/releases/latest/download/CrossingTime-android.apk>.
Open the link on the phone, open the downloaded file, and press **Install**.
If the phone says it is not allowed, press **Settings**, turn on **Allow from
this source**, go back and press **Install**.

Vibration is on from the start. To test it, open **Accessibility** from the
person button and press **Test haptic pulse**.

Android phones vary. If you get stuck, ask your instructor. It is a one-minute
fix in person.

**The session code.** Each time CrossingTime opens it asks for a session
code. The four practice activities open after you type the code your
instructor gives you at the session. Press **Later** to look around first:
Help, Accessibility and crossing times work without the code.

**Coming soon: Google Play.** The app is being set up on Google Play. When
your instructor sends you a Google Play link, tap it and press **Install**.
Nothing to allow, nothing to download by hand, the same as any other app. Until
then the file above is the way.

## If you have no smartphone, or you cannot install

You can still try the app in a web browser on any phone or computer. The
address is:

**<https://azizulhaque.me/crossingtime/app/>**

Everything works except vibration. On an iPhone, Safari can add it to the home
screen: press **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Which version do I have, and how do I report a problem?

Open the app, press **Help**, and read **About**. The current version is
**build 18**. If it says an older build, update: on iPhone open TestFlight and
press **Update**; on Android download the file above again and install it over
the old one (your practice history stays); in a browser, reload the page.

If something goes wrong or is confusing, tell us. Either tell your instructor,
or write it here (a free GitHub account is needed):
<https://github.com/meazizul/crossingtime/issues/new>

Say which phone you have, the build from **About**, what you did, what
happened, and what you expected. If you use VoiceOver or TalkBack, a screen
recording helps most.

## Your privacy

The app has no account and no sign-in, and does not collect any data. Your
practice history is kept on your own phone, and in your phone's own backup if
it is on. You can delete it at any time from **Progress**, then **Delete all my
practice history**; the app asks you to confirm first.

The full privacy policy is here:
<https://github.com/meazizul/crossingtime/blob/main/PRIVACY.md>
