# Gap Sense — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Gap Sense is a research tool from Stevens Institute of Technology that helps
blind, low-vision and DeafBlind travellers build an intuitive sense of how long
a street crossing takes, using sound and vibration rather than numbers.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Gap Sense has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, and no servers of its own. It never transmits data off your
device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings and practice history** (margin, cue preferences, and the
  timings you record) are stored only in the app's local storage on your
  phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Microphone.** If you grant microphone access, the app measures how loud
  the surroundings are so it can flag a measurement taken in noise too loud to
  be representative. The audio is analysed in real time on your device, and is
  never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. You can decline microphone access
  and every other feature still works.

## Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs that collect data. During testing it is
distributed through Apple's TestFlight, which is governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
