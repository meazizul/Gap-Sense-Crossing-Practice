# Gap Sense — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Gap Sense is a research tool from Stevens Institute of Technology that helps
blind, low-vision and DeafBlind travelers build an intuitive sense of how long
a street crossing takes, using sound and vibration rather than numbers.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Gap Sense does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no
sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Your data
never reaches the people who make the app, or anyone else.

## Where your data is kept

- **On your phone.** Your settings and practice history (margin, cue
  preferences, and the timings you record) are kept in the app's storage on
  your phone.
- **In your phone's own backup, if it is on.** Like other apps' data, Gap
  Sense's data is included when your phone backs itself up to your own
  account:
  - **iPhone:** iCloud Backup (Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then
    iCloud Backup).
  - **Android:** Backup by Google One (Settings, then System, on some phones
    Google, then Backup). The backup is encrypted with your phone's PIN,
    pattern or password on Android 9 and later.

  The backup is kept by Apple or Google in your account. Only you can use it,
  to restore your phone. The people who make Gap Sense cannot see it.
- **Web version.** Kept only in the browser on that device.
- **No microphone.** The app does not use the microphone at all, and does not
  ask for access to it.
- **Instructor mobile number, email and student code.** Instructor setup can
  hold an instructor's mobile number, an instructor's email address and a
  short student code (letters and digits). They are kept with your settings.
  They appear in the setup link an instructor shares, and the code appears at
  the top of the practice report, so that nothing needs a name. The app never
  stores or sends a name.
- **Sending a report or a setup link.** Nothing is sent automatically. When you
  choose to send, the app shows you the report first, then does only what you
  pick: opens Messages with the report (addressed to the instructor's number
  when one is set), opens your mail app, hands the report or a spreadsheet
  file to your phone's share menu, or shows the report on your screen as a
  code for the instructor to scan in person. In every case it goes only where
  you send it, and the code is read only by the camera that scans it. Nothing
  passes through any Gap Sense server; there is none.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete all my practice history** (in Progress) deletes your history from
  your phone at once. Your phone's backup keeps only its latest copy, so the
  history is gone from the backup after your phone's next backup, usually
  within a day. Your settings, crossing times and instructor setup stay.
- **Deleting the app** deletes its data from your phone. A backup made before
  then still has it until your phone's next backup.

## Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs that collect data. During testing it is
distributed through Apple's TestFlight, which is governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If your
phone's backup is on, Apple (iCloud) or Google (Backup by Google One) keeps it
in your account, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
or [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
