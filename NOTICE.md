# Notice: origins and collaboration

Gap Sense — Crossing Practice began as a copy of **Cindi Lashinsky's CrossingTime
(Streets) repository**, her original proof of concept for teaching the timing
of uncontrolled street crossings. That prototype established the idea at the
heart of this app: feedback as felt duration, never as a number of seconds.

The app has since been refined and expanded through ongoing collaboration
between Cindi Lashinsky, Orientation and Mobility Specialist, who originated
the design and continues to set its requirements and test it; Dona
Sauerburger, Orientation and Mobility Specialist, whose work on crossing
streets without traffic control informs the method; and Azizul Haque, who
builds the app at Stevens Institute of Technology under the supervision of
Prof. Jonggi Hong.

Design decisions attributed in the code and documentation to Cindi Lashinsky
are hers; where a rule came from the development side, the documentation says
so.
