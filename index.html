CrossingTime

Crossing practice, for use with your O&M Specialist.

This page is the one link you need. It stays the same; what is behind it is kept up to date. Install at home, before your session.

Coming to a session?

Install the app now, on the phone you will bring. The practice activities open with a short code you receive in the room. Everything else in the app works straight away. Bring headphones.

iPhone

Get CrossingTime for iPhone
  1. Press the button. TestFlight opens. If you do not have TestFlight yet, it takes you to get it first; then press the button again.
  2. Press Accept, then Install.

Already have it? Open TestFlight and press Update. The app may still show its earlier name, Gap Sense, until the next update.

Android

Get CrossingTime for Android
  1. Press the button. The file downloads. Press Download if the phone asks.
  2. Open the downloaded file and press Install. If the phone says it is not allowed, press Settings, allow this source, then go back and press Install.

This button always gives the newest build; installing over an older one keeps your data. A Google Play version is on its way; when it is ready, this button will take you to Google Play instead and the allow step goes away.

Any browser, nothing to install

Open CrossingTime here

Open it once with internet; after that it works offline too. Everything works except vibration.

After installing

  1. Open the app. The first screen explains that it is for use with an O&M Specialist. Press I understand.
  2. Open Help to read how each activity works, or Accessibility (the person button) for text size, themes, speech and vibration.
  3. Your instructor enters your crossing times, in person or by sending you a link.

Full guides: getting the app on your phone and how to use it. Questions: ask your instructor.
