    Sounds

    Every sound means something. Play them here any time.

    For clients

    • 1. Measure my crossing. Walk the street a few times, tapping at each point. This is what everything else is compared to.
    • 2. Practise my timing. Tap Begin, then tap again when you think you would have arrived. You hear the difference rather than being told a number.
    • 3. Compare practice. The app plays a gap. Decide whether it was longer or shorter than the time you need.
    • 4. At the street. The same judgement with real traffic.

    Never count. The point is to feel the length of time, not to measure it.

    For instructors

    • Setting up. Crossing times can be measured in the app, or typed in Settings, or sent to the client as a link from Settings → Share time settings.
    • Margin of error. Defaults to 0.40 s. Historically 0.50 s has been taught. Turn on the adaptive margin in Progress to have it shape difficulty automatically.
    • Feedback modes. Audio, visual, or both, under Accessibility. Visual-only supports Deaf clients; vibration supports DeafBlind clients in the native app.
    • Progress. The client can show you the Progress screen, or copy a written report to send you. Data never leaves their device unless they send it.
    • At the street. Sample the quiet before starting so the app can flag measurements taken when it was too noisy to be representative.

    What the sounds mean

    Your own taps

    This plays back where you tapped.

    Close enough

    A short, bright chime. You were within the margin.

    Outside the margin

    A longer, low, buzzing pulse. You were too far off.

    The important part

    What matters is the gap between your tap and the reference sound. The further apart they are, the further off you were. Never count seconds — listen for the distance between them.
