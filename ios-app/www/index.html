For students

VoiceOver: on the iPhone app each answer screen is a direct touch area, and VoiceOver's cursor lands on it as the screen opens. Turn on Direct Touch for Gap Sense once (VoiceOver rotor, Direct Touch), and swipes and taps there work the same as with VoiceOver off. Back, in the top-left corner, is outside the area: touch it, hear "Back", double-tap. TalkBack: swipe with two fingers on the answer screen, or double-tap to tap.

  • Your crossing times come from your O&M Specialist. They time you crossing and set the times in Instructor setup, or send you a link that sets them.
  • 1. Practice my timing. Tap Begin, then tap again when you think you would have arrived. You hear the difference rather than being told a number.
  • 2. Comparison preparation. Press Ready. When the sound starts, stop it when you think your crossing time is up.
  • 3. Comparison practice. The app plays a sample warning time. Decide whether it was longer, shorter, or about the same as your crossing time, then feel the difference.
  • 4. At the street. The same judgment with real vehicles.

Never count. The point is to feel the length of time, not to measure it.

For instructors

  • Setting up. Time at least three crossings and use the longest. Enter the first-half and full-street times in Instructor setup. There you also choose which activities and options the student sees, then send everything to the student's phone as one link.
  • Margin of error. You choose and set it. The app defaults to 0.40 seconds; 0.50 seconds has historically been taught with a stopwatch. There is no research yet on the right value.
  • Directions. A vehicle approaching from the left is compared with the first-half time; from the right, with the full-street time.
  • Feedback modes. Audio, visual, or both, under Accessibility. Visual-only supports Deaf students; vibration supports DeafBlind students in the installed app.
  • Progress. The student can show you the Progress screen, or send you a written report. Data never leaves their device unless they send it.
  • Experimental. The adaptive margin is off by default and unproven; turn it on in Instructor setup only to try it.

What the sounds mean

This is your time

Your own sound. In a replay it marks your taps, and in the comparison replays it marks where your crossing time ends.

Close enough

A short, bright chime. You were within the margin, or your answer was right.

Outside the margin

A longer, low, buzzing pulse. You were too far off, or your answer was wrong.

A sample warning time

In the comparison activities: one steady sound, the same loudness from start to end. Its length is the warning time.

Get ready

In Comparison preparation, the app says "Get ready" when you press Ready. Then wait for the sound.

The important part

What matters is the distance between your sound and the other sound. The further apart they are, the further off you were. Never count seconds; listen for the distance between them.
